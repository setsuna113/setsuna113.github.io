// research

Racer: Recoverable KV-Cache Management for Multi-Turn Agents

Aug–Sep 2026 · SJTU · Prof. Shengzhong Liu

A wrong action taken under KV compression changes the environment and the error accumulates over later turns. Racer detects risky drafts before an action executes, restores the original evidence and regenerates. Co-first author, under review at ICLR 2027.

+43.7% task success on BFCL Long Context (relative) · 1.67× the task throughput of full context under concurrent serving

  • KV-cache compression

ShapeFlow: Serving Agentic Workflows with Late-Bound Representations

May–Jul 2026 · SJTU · Prof. Shengzhong Liu

Deep-research agents decode a summary of every retrieved page. ShapeFlow is a scheduler that chooses under engine load between per-page summaries and a decision model that outputs only span IDs.

CPU-selected spans in place of page summaries: −67% vLLM busy time per task on BrowseComp-Plus
  • vLLM
  • scheduler
  • BrowseComp-Plus
  • open_deep_research

Coupled Muon: Product-Space Orthogonalization for Transformer Optimizers

Mar–May 2026 · SJTU AI · Yiting Chen, Prof. Junchi Yan

Muon orthogonalizes each matrix's update separately, leaving the Q–K and V–O product updates uncontrolled. Coupled Muon orthogonalizes the product's update.

0.007–0.024 nats lower validation loss than Muon on RoPE dense and MoE variants · 960 runs
  • Muon
  • Newton–Schulz
  • MoE
